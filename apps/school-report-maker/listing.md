## Full description
The teacher side of School Report Maker, used by Ugandan secondary schools.

This app is for class teachers. It works completely offline: enter marks on your phone in class, then hand them to the school console by QR code or file. No data bundle needed, and no waiting for the school network.

WHAT IT DOES
• Enter marks per subject and assessment: Beginning of Term, Mid Term, End of Term
• Only your school's learners ever appear, and marks from another school are refused
• Works offline for the whole term, then syncs to the school computer
• Uganda A–E grading, aggregates, division and automatic remarks
• Report cards print from the school console, not the phone

WHO IT IS FOR
Class teachers and heads of department in Ugandan secondary schools.

Privacy: the app requests internet only for optional school sync and updates.
