## Full description
DeryAccount runs your shop even when the internet doesn't.

It is a complete point of sale and bookkeeping system for Ugandan shops, built to work with no connection at all. Sell, receipt, track stock and close the day without waiting for data.

WHAT IT DOES
• Tap-to-sell product tiles with stock left on every tile
• Cash, mobile money and credit sales, with customer balances and repayments
• Receipts printed on cheap Bluetooth thermal printers, or saved as PDF
• Full books of account: cash book, petty cash, bank and MoMo books, ledgers, trial balance, income statement and balance sheet. Every sale posts itself
• Stock: purchase, cost price, write-offs, low-stock warnings
• Barcode scanning with the phone camera
• End-of-day Z report with cash, MoMo and credit split
• Multi-branch, backup and restore to your own storage

WHO IT IS FOR
Shops, pharmacies, hardware stores, agro-vets, butchers, salons, bars and supermarkets.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT
No subscription and no internet requirement. Your data stays on your phone, and it exports whenever you want.
